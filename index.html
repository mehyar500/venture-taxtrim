Questions

What's the deadline?

The NYC Tax Commission filing deadline for Class 1 properties is typically mid-March each year — it moves slightly, so your packet includes that year's exact deadline pulled from the Tax Commission calendar each January. Miss it and the number locks in for the year.

Which homes does this work for?

Class 1 properties: 1-, 2-, and 3-family homes in Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island, and the Bronx. Condos and co-ops (Class 2) use a different valuation method — the check will tell you if your property isn't a fit.

Where does the data come from?

NYC Open Data: the Department of Finance property assessment roll, the rolling calendar sales file, and PLUTO for locations. Every result card stamps its data vintage.

What does the $39 packet include?

Your comparable-sales analysis with ranked comps, a pre-filled Tax Commission complaint narrative drafted from your exact numbers, step-by-step filing instructions, the deadline calendar, and a printable PDF. Plus an optional $49/year Assessment Watch that monitors your BBL and re-runs every January.

Is my address stored or shared?

Your address is used to look up public assessment records and compute your verdict. We never sell your data. Email is only used for your results and the packet you buy — one-click unsubscribe on everything, always. See our privacy policy.
